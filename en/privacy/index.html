Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-12

Projectteamforyou (“we”) makes and runs OmniSort (the Windows app, the Android app and the website omnisort.win). This policy says, plainly, what information we handle and how.

1. In one line

We do not take your files, file names or folder paths. The apps do all of their work on your own device.

  • There is no sign-up or login, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. The thin strip at the top (labeled “Ad”) is just a link introducing Projectteamforyou’s other programs, apps and games; a web browser opens on your device only when you tap it.
  • The Android app has no internet permission at all, so it technically cannot talk to the outside.
  • The website counts visits and downloads only by date and country (two letters). It keeps nothing that identifies you.

2. What this covers

This policy applies to:

  • OmniSort for Windows
  • OmniSort for Android
  • The website omnisort.win
  • The download address dl.omnisort.win

3. What the apps handle on your device (Windows and Android)

The information below is used only on your device, to plan the sorting, move the files and undo it. It is not sent to us.

  • The name, size, modified time and extension of the files in the folders you choose to sort — used to work out where each file should go.
  • The date a photo was taken (EXIF) — read only when you choose the taken-date as the basis of the date folders.
  • The first few bytes of a file — read to warn you when a file’s extension does not match what it really is (for example, a program pretending to be a photo). The content is not stored.
  • The activity log — where each moved file was and where it went, and its name. It is stored in a database on your device so that you can undo. You can clear it in the app, and it disappears when you uninstall the app.
  • Settings — language, sorting options and the rules you created.

The Android app only reaches folders you pick yourself in the system folder picker. If you grant “All files access”, it still works in the same way, only inside the folders that permission reaches.

4. What the apps send off your device

  • Android app: nothing. The app has no internet permission. (Tapping a link such as “Privacy Policy” in Settings, or the strip at the top, opens your device’s web browser; what happens after that is the browser’s communication.)
  • Windows app: for the update check (on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings) it fetches the release list over https once a day (one GET request). The app sends no information; the request carries the app name and version number. Fetching notices and viewing the download count are off by default; if you turn them on, they fetch public files in the same way. The strip at the top fetches nothing; clicking it opens your default web browser.

In no case are file names, paths or counts sent out.

5. The website (omnisort.win)

  • Cookies: we do not set cookies. There is no sign-up or login.
  • Visit count: when you open a page, only a count for one line — the date and a country code (for example KR) — is raised. Your IP address, browser name and where you came from are not stored. A line is the sum of many people, so one person cannot be traced from it.
  • Download count: we count only by date, country code, form (single file / folder build / command line) and version. It does not identify individuals in the same way. Robots and prefetches are not counted.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: used to see which pages are read and how much. It uses no cookies and no device fingerprint; visit statistics such as the referrer, browser type and country are passed to and processed by Cloudflare, Inc.
  • Hosting: the site is served from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and downloads and the front of the site go through Cloudflare. They may process access records (such as IP addresses) under their own policies for operation and security. We do not use those records to identify anyone.
  • Offline storage: so that the site opens quickly, parts of the pages are stored in the visitor’s browser (a service worker). That data stays on the visitor’s device.

6. Disclosure to third parties and transfers abroad

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not provide it to third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • When you use the website, access information may be processed on the servers of the providers in section 5 (located in the United States). While you use the apps, information about your files does not leave your device.

7. Retention and deletion

  • The information in the apps is all on your device. You can erase it by clearing the activity log in the app or by uninstalling the app. (Android: Settings > Apps > OmniSort > Storage > Clear data)
  • The website’s totals are aggregate numbers that cannot identify anyone, and we keep them to run the service.

8. Your rights and how to contact us

We hold practically no personal information. Even so, if you want to see, correct or delete it, or to stop its processing, write to the address below. We will check and answer without delay.

Privacy contact: Projectteamforyou · projectteamforyou@gmail.com

9. Children

OmniSort is not a service aimed at children, and we do not collect children’s personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. Important changes are also announced at the top of the home page.
